Who we work with

We work best when the problem is operational.

Mid-market B2B businesses who need software their team or clients use daily — not marketing assets.

✓  Good fit

  • You're running a process on spreadsheets or a generic tool that doesn't quite fit
  • You need a mobile app for a field team, driver, or warehouse operative
  • You're building a B2B SaaS product and need engineering alongside product thinking
  • You have a clear problem and want someone who can propose the solution — not just execute a spec

✗  Not a good fit

  • Consumer apps — social, entertainment, games
  • Marketing websites, branding, and design-only projects
  • E-commerce and Shopify builds
  • Projects without a defined problem ("we need an app" with no use case)
